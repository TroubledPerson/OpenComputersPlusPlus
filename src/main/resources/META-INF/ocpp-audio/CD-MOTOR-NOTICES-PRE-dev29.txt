The dev.28 loop uses source seconds 7.0-8.5, gain 1.75 and a 100 ms equal-power
tail/head blend, producing a 1.4-second mono 22050 Hz Vorbis cached loop. Its
measured spectral balance and level are closer to the accepted startup ending.
The startup and seek assets were not regenerated. Previous loop bytes/provenance
remain preserved in earlier releases and verification backups.

No independent listening, Minecraft or audio-device test was performed. Numeric
