# OpenComputers++ dev.33

Tray open/close gain is 0.90, increased from 0.75 (+1.58 dB). Accepted B, startup 2.51 s, loop 2.4 s, recorded stop and tray audio bytes are identical to dev32. Other gains, pitch and audio timing remain unchanged. Higher configured volume, multiple drives and other game sounds can still sum or clamp.

The drive now distinguishes an unrelated temporary-filesystem disconnect during normal computer shutdown from genuine drive/owner connectivity loss. Last-owner shutdown or in-game force-off requests the recorded stop once. Real disconnect/unload cancels immediately. Ejection is classified using the authoritative previous motor state: a stopped drive plays tray-open only; a spinning drive requests stop plus tray-open once. Ejecting during an existing stop retains its gain/deadline. Repeated/stale events do not restart cues.

Media remains loaded internally but its filesystem is unavailable while off or spinning up. After tray closure and motor activation, readiness requires 51 server ticks. This is a simulation and does not await client audio or claim physical playback completion. Power loss, eject, replacement, connectivity loss and unload cancel readiness and stale handles. Reload requires fresh readiness; disc files, identity, seal/completion metadata and EEPROM contents are preserved.

The common Lua bootstrap waits up to 6 simulated uptime seconds for starting optical drives before invoking the installed EEPROM BIOS. Computers with no starting CD retain their existing boot path. No EEPROM is flashed or rewritten. Timeout/unavailable media returns to the existing BIOS behavior. Duplicate identity/backing-data reopen failure stays unavailable for that activation rather than escaping the tick or repeatedly retrying. A later power cycle or replacement can retry. Repeated close is ownership-safe and cannot release another active mount's reservation.

384 regression plus 13 packaged-loader tests passed with no failures/errors/skips.71 focused and 75 actual reobfuscated-JAR policy/ownership/readiness cases overlap this coverage. The real network-graph fixture reproduces temporary-node removal before the stopped notification; world/power and other integration fixtures remain synthetic. Initial fixture construction and sender errors are preserved with their reports and remain charged. No tests were removed to hide failures.

Both compiled Forge entrypoint annotations and metadata report 1.8.10-ocpp-dev.33. Main Java/Scala were verified unchanged during final validation. The source ZIP is byte-bound to release inputs. Native originals, 48 connected textures, previous builds and accepted sound provenance remain preserved. Audio attribution remains Marcus Dellicompagni / PoundSoundUK, source 718390, CC BY 4.0; existing modification notices are included.

Normal OpenOS CD support remains separate from Tier 7 SuperBIOS. The optional offline VM addon is unchanged; no QEMU/guest execution, external IPC or real disk attachment was added. No Minecraft world, GL context or audio device was run, and nothing was deployed. Live mounting/boot timing, shutdown stop audibility and tray balance require owner testing. Historical unsampled-write uncertainty remains disclosed.
